Bio

I am currently a Project Postdoc Researcher in the Echizen Lab at the National Institute of Informatics (NII), Japan. My name in Chinese and Japanese is 冉 維航 / ゼン イコウ.

My research focuses on making AI systems more robust, secure, and trustworthy — especially when they operate under adversarial or unsafe conditions. I am broadly interested in understanding how intelligent systems fail, how such failures can be systematically evaluated, and how we can build models that remain reliable as they move from perception and language toward interaction with the physical world.

A few topics I am particularly interested in are:

Adversarial Robustness
How can we understand and mitigate the vulnerabilities of learning systems when their inputs, environments, or assumptions are intentionally manipulated?
Multimodal Safety & Security
How do safety and security risks change when models jointly process language, vision, and other modalities, and how should we evaluate their behavior under malicious or deceptive inputs?
Embodied / VLA Safety
As AI systems begin to perceive, reason, and act in the physical world, how can we ensure that they complete tasks safely while remaining robust to adversarial instructions and environmental disturbances?

I received my Ph.D. in Information Science and Technology from the University of Tokyo in September 2026, where I was a member of the OSCARS Lab and supervised by Prof. Yinqiang Zheng. Previously, I received my M.E. in Socio-Cultural Environmental Study from the University of Tokyo, supervised by Prof. Ryosuke Shibasaki, Prof. Xuan Song, and Prof. Wei Yuan. Before that, I received my Bachelor's degree from Wuhan University.

Prospective Students

NII has long-standing partnerships with universities and research institutes around the world. If you are interested in a research visit or internship at NII, please first check whether your home institution has a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with NII by consulting the list of partner institutions.

If your institution has an MOU with NII:
Please apply to the NII International Internship Program through your home institution.

If your institution does not have an MOU with NII:
You are welcome to inquire about possible research visits or internships on an individual basis. Please email Prof. Isao Echizen or me directly and attach your CV.
